# 4.13 Multi-currency banks

- **Status:** `done`
- **Goal:** each bank has its own currency, every shown balance names that currency, and `/all` / `/total` list one sum per currency.
- **Depends on:** `banks.currency` from `3.6` (already `TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'USD'`).

Do **not** implement this umbrella as one session. `4.13.1`, `4.13.2`, and `4.13.3` are done. Next is [`4.14.1`](4.14.1-fx-rates.md).

This umbrella shipped **one balance per bank**, and `/currency` relabels that balance without converting. [`4.14.2`](4.14.2-currency-balances.md) replaces that model with one balance per currency and removes `/currency`. Do not build that here.

Product rules for this step and [`4.14`](4.14-fx-transfer.md) are locked in [`requirements.md`](../requirements.md) (Currencies and FX). Sub-steps restate the part they build.

| Step | File | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4.13.1 | [Currency model](4.13.1-currency-model.md) | Validate codes, format amounts, backfill `USD`, store last currency |
| 4.13.2 | [Currency display](4.13.2-currency-display.md) | Every balance shows its currency; totals grouped, never converted |
| 4.13.3 | [Currency picker](4.13.3-currency-picker.md) | Choose a currency on `/newbank`; change it later with `/currency` |

**Not here:** exchange rates and cross-currency `/transfer` → [`4.14`](4.14-fx-transfer.md). A converted grand total is not a later step; this product does not convert `/all` or `/total`.
